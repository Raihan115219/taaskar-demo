TAASKAR

Interactive prototype

A route-based peer-to-peer task marketplace for Bengaluru. Everything runs in your browser with sample data.

  1. Log in as the Sender (demo@taaskar.in) and post a task from Create.
  2. Open a second window, log in as the Runner (runner@taaskar.in) and accept it from Explore.
  3. The Runner updates each step in Track; the Sender sees it live and releases payment.
  4. Password for both: Demo@1234
